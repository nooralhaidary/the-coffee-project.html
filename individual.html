What is The Coffee Project?

The Coffee Project follows coffee from a wild plant in the highlands of Ethiopia to one of the most popular drinks in the world. Find out where it came from, how much of it the world drinks today, and the cafés and companies that made it famous.

Every fact on the site is backed by a source, listed at the bottom of each page.
